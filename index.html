01. About Me

I'm an ML Engineer who turns research-grade speech models into reliable production systems. My day-to-day is conversational AI: Speech-to-Text → LLM → Text-to-Speech, served on Kubernetes with Triton and vLLM.

At NoBroker (Convozen.ai) I built the voicebot pipelines and a full conversation simulator that lets us replay, mutate, and stress-test agent behavior at scale before any change touches a real call.

I also ship the analytics pipeline behind it — call summaries, intent breakdowns, latency dashboards. Before this, I worked at DRDO on QUIC protocol security and disclosed 2 connection-migration vulnerabilities.
